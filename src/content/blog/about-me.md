# Welcome to the blog

Welcome. I'm a generalist backend developer. I'll write here about APIs, architecture, databases, infrastructure, and everything around building services that actually work.

## Who I am

I work mainly on the server side: designing and building services that consume and expose data. Day-to-day that means deciding how that layer talks to others, how it persists information, and how it stays alive in production.

## My stack

Tools I work with regularly:

- **Backend:** Java, Kotlin, PHP, and JavaScript when needed.
- **Databases:** SQL and NoSQL, with clear schemas and versioned migrations.
- **Messaging:** RabbitMQ for communication between services.
- **Architecture:** hexagonal as the preferred approach to keep the domain decoupled.
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD with reproducible environments.
- **Observability:** logs, metrics, and traces designed early, not bolted on at the end.

## What you'll find here

- **Real practice:** decisions I've made and the reasoning behind them.
- **Lessons:** mistakes, shortcuts, patterns worth sharing.
- **Technical notes:** short pieces on specific problems.
- **Experiments:** things I try in my own projects, including this very website.

## How to reach me

Find me on GitHub or send me an email. I'll be posting regularly, thanks for reading.
